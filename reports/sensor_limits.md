# Permissible depth-sensor error

Generated by `cattle3d sensor`. The deployable 3-D ensemble is trained on clean clouds (5-fold CV by animal, 3 seeds) and scored on clouds degraded as a worse camera would record them. Tolerance: the MAE increase over clean clouds, upper bound of the paired 95 % bootstrap CI, stays within 2.5 kg and no animal is lost. 'After recalibration' fits slope and intercept on 20 random reference animals of the degraded herd (200 draws) and scores the rest.
